Bob Corrigan

Redmond, WA

Bob Corrigan is an employer benefits professional, pro disc golfer, poet, and retro film photographer in Redmond, Washington, with two cats and a penchant for vintage RC models.

He writes poems at falling spoon, short pieces about light, the body, movement, and paying attention, including the 2026 collection sliding in the light.

He shoots 35mm film on an Olympus Trip 35 and shares it at Secret Vending Machine, a warm, lo-fi gallery and zine.

On the course, Bob plays disc golf tournaments around Washington (PDGA #316354). The photo wall on the home page and his field notes are mostly that.
