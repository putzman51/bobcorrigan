About Bob

Employer benefits professional, pro disc golfer, poet and film photographer in Redmond, Washington. Two cats. A penchant for vintage RC models.

On the course, Bob plays PDGA tournaments around Washington and beyond as PDGA #316354. The home page tracks every rated round, and the field notes tell the stories behind them.

Off the course, he works in employer benefits, writes poems and shoots 35mm film on an Olympus Trip 35.
