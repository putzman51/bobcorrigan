Pier Park Play-off!

C-Tier · Pier Park DGC · Portland, OR

Rating933

Pier Park is one of the best courses in the PNW, and this stretch of woods is a treat. I stayed near par all round: 3 birds, 1 bogey, 2 under, tied for first.

My first play-off. First person to drop a stroke loses. We both hit edge-of-C1 putts to stay alive on the first hole, went around 1, 2 and 3 twice, then moved to 8, 9 and 10. On 10 I made par and Sean found a bogey. I stepped up for the tap in and closed out the round.

I shot a 933 and it felt repeatable. Playing the night before and doing field work that morning helped, so I'm keeping that routine.

A play-off is a kind of pressure I had not felt before. I love competing and I love winning. LFG.

"I stepped up for the tap in and closed out the round."
Result 1st Place · MA3 (Play-off)
No. 12 SeaTac · Whitmarsh · South Fork
10–12 Apr 2026 League + 2× C-Tier

6 Rounds in 3 Days

SeaTac · Whitmarsh (Mt. Vernon) · South Fork

Rated874·854·947·879·862

What a weekend! The best parts were off the card: new people, good friends, great meals. Mount Vernon and Burlington have serious food. Don't sleep on "Big Scoop," and Train Wreck Bar & Grill makes an amazing Reuben.

The SeaTac league round was ordinary. I shot near my rating and learned a few things.

Friday was my first look at Whitmarsh, a legendary little wooded course. My only goal for the practice round was to learn where the bogeys live.

Saturday's first round was flat and left me on the chase card in 7th. In round two I avoided the bogeys, found birds late, and hit a 120 foot throw-in. My friend was waiting on the last hole to tell me I was in the lead: just par it and it's mine. I threw an easy, smooth drive to within 30 feet. Par was all I needed.

Sharing that with Brian and the Bok Squad was a joy.

"My friend was waiting on the last hole to let me know I was in the lead. Just par this last hole and it's mine."

No. 11 Kayak Point · NXNW4 · Red Course
4–5 Apr 2026 PDGA B-Tier · Discraft

Kayak Point (NXNW4)

NXNW4, Sponsored by Discraft · Kayak Point · Red Course

RoundsR1: 897·R2: 877

Crisp spring weather, sunshine, light wind, and the biggest field I've joined. The MA3 winner averaged 933 over two rounds. That's solid game!

I only beat one guy on each card, but in a field this size that was 12 players and 58.5 points. I'm on pace for the 1000 that gets me into AM Worlds '27.

Two shots I'll remember, both late in round one. A 50 foot death putt in the wind that got the whole card cheering. Then on 16, a 160 foot Fireball upshot that turned exactly as hard as it had to.

Such a simple and pure joy to ring up the chains and stop in the basket.

"Such a simple and pure joy to ring up the chains and stop in the basket."

No. 10 Sunnyside Up · Sunnyside Park · Pullman, WA
28–29 Mar 2026 PDGA B-Tier · W · Ace

Sunnyside Up (First MA3 Win!)

Sunnyside Up B-Tier · Sunnyside Park · Pullman, WA

RoundsR1: 866·R2: 906·R3: 916

My first time playing MA3, and I won by 3 strokes. It was close to the end. On the last tee I was at E and second place was at +1.

The last hole is a downhill island, smaller than C1. I had missed it in practice and was 1 for 2 in the rounds. I grabbed my Envy and told myself to stay relaxed. I thought of a friend I used to play with, pretended he was standing down by the basket, and tossed it down to him. It landed 10 feet left of the pin, about 200 feet down.

Then I checked the score for the first time and knew I had it. Michael came over to shake my hand and I gave him a hug. What a day!

It started with a skip-ace on hole 17, the hole I sponsored. Isaac McGonigal witnessed it and inked me up. I wrote "hole 2" on the disc because I started each round on 16. I'm keeping it that way. Hole what? Does it even matter?!

"I thought of a friend, pretended he was standing down by the basket, and tossed it down to him."
Result 1st Place · MA3

Star Frame Sunday

Casual Round Meetup · Jackson Golf Course · Seattle, WA

I jumped on a meetup for 18 holes at Jackson, a ball golf and disc golf hybrid. Everyone shares the fairways, and the baskets sit just past the greens, which play as hazards. Fun elevation, fun shot shapes, pristine grounds.

My cardmate was finding great lines. We celebrated a star frame after both of us fully parked the same forehand on 17. A chill way to grab a few birds on a Sunday morning.

"We celebrated a star frame after both of us fully parked the same forehand on 17."

No. 07 Shelton Springs Disc Golf · Shelton, WA
21 Mar 2026 PDGA C-Tier

Shelton Springs

Shelton Springs Disc Golf · Shelton, WA

RoundsR1: 859·R2: 878

A legendary course I've watched on coverage for years. It gave me the chills when I parked the signature island hole and tapped in for bird.

I played the white tees, then the red. The whites are much harder. I was on the MA1 card in round one and watched a 22 year old local shred them. I laced a few fairways between the bogeys.

Round two was my shot at redemption. I finished under par without knowing it, cutting a Fireball through windows I had never tried before.

Both rounds were under my rating, but that's the game. The lesson: I need more putting. I've been working on upshots, and now it's time to put the two together.

"It gave me the chills when I parked the signature island hole and tapped in for bird."
Result 4th Place · MA40

Behold, the Rain Wizard

Slingin' Singles 4 · Emerald City Discs

RoundsR1: 930·R2: 842

Round one was a revelation: 1 under par, clean, everything clicking.

Then lunch happened, and a cold, steady rain turned the course into a slip-and-slide. Traction? Gone. Dry towels? Soaked by the halfway point. Morale? Fading.

The 88 point swing between rounds was humbling. Even in rain I should hold my rating better. The card stayed calm and nobody complained much. The Rain Wizard title is aspirational at best.

"The Rain Wizard title is aspirational at best."

No. 02 Discmania Disc Golf Park · Miami, AZ
31 Jan 2026 PDGA C-Tier · Ace

Copper in the Mountains

NADGT C Tier 2 Rounder · Discmania DGP · Miami, AZ

RoundsR1: 853·R2: 943

Worth the drive for the signature hole alone: a hanging basket in front of a big dump truck bed, in the heart of copper country. The course is short, but the mandos and OB punish lazy shots.

And then it happened. My first sanctioned ace, on the signature hole. An official 1 now living on my PDGA stats forever. Garrett on our card aced one too, and we both lost our minds.

I finished a few strokes short of first, behind a guy who played beautifully steady all day. Todd the tournament director and his wife Anissa were incredible hosts. The Mexican restaurant afterward may be the best I've ever had. The whole trip was a 10.

"An official 1 now living on my PDGA stats forever."
Result 2nd Place · MA40

SeaTac Shenanigans

Say it Ain't Snow · SeaTac DGC · SeaTac, WA

RoundsR1: 914·R2: 904

Pretty weather, my 4th tournament, and somehow I won the whole thing.

I opened both rounds with a double bogey, like it's a tradition now, then tightened up and played real golf. It stayed close to the end. I finished one stroke better in each round, so the last two holes could have gone either way.

Two highlights. A throw-in from about 100 feet on hole 6: the disc disappears behind some trees and you just hear chains. And a 45 foot putt, the distance I practice obsessively.

I spent the prize money on P2 D-Line putters. The most disc golf way to reinvest your winnings.

"The disc disappears behind some trees and you just hear chains."
Result 1st Place · MA40
